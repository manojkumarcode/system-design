# 09 — AI Engineering

Interview preparation track for AI/LLM-backed systems, aimed at the
system-design and hands-on-depth bar expected at senior/staff/principal
level: not "what is an LLM," but "how do you design, operate, and debug
an AI-backed production system."

## Chapters

### [Chapter 01 — Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)](chapter-01-rag/)

The full RAG pipeline end to end, from ingestion through generation, with
a Spring AI implementation, production architecture, performance,
security, and a consolidated interview question bank.
